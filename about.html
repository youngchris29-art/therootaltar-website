Meet the Founder

Who Is Starr Blayze?

Spiritual Coach & Tarot Reader

Starr Blayze is the founder and guiding force behind The Root Altar. With a deep-rooted connection to spiritual practices and a passion for empowering others, Starr has served over 150 clients through personalized tarot readings, healing rituals, and coaching sessions.

Specializing in tarot, candle magic, cord cutting, and personal growth coaching, Starr brings a unique blend of intuitive insight and practical wisdom to every session. Their approach is grounded in ethics, compassion, and a commitment to helping you reclaim your power.

Whether you're navigating heartbreak, seeking clarity on your path, or ready to level up spiritually — Starr and The Root Altar team are here to walk alongside you.

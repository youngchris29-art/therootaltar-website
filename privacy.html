Privacy Policy

How we handle your information at The Root Altar.

Last updated: March 9, 2026

What Information Do We Collect?

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide to us through our contact form or when booking services. This may include your name, email address, and any details you share in your message. We do not use tracking cookies or sell your data to third parties.

How Do We Use Your Information?

Your information is used solely to respond to your inquiries, process bookings, and provide our spiritual services. We may use your email to communicate about your session, send confirmations, or follow up on your experience. We will never send unsolicited marketing emails without your consent.

Do We Share Your Information?

No. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties. Your privacy and the sacredness of our client relationships are paramount. Information shared during readings and sessions is kept strictly confidential.

How Do We Protect Your Information?

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to contact us immediately if you have concerns about data security.

What About Third-Party Links?

Our website may contain links to third-party websites such as our TikTok, Instagram, or external booking platform. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

How Do We Use Cookies?

The Root Altar website does not use tracking cookies or analytics services. We believe in a clean, respectful browsing experience. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and notify visitors accordingly.

How Can You Contact Us About Privacy?

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at Contact@TheRootAltar.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
